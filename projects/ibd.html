Overview

Turning multi-source data into usable research tools

For this masters capstone, I worked with BC Children's Hospital Research Institute to turn raw clinical, dietary, biomarker, and mycobiome data into reproducible data products. The work supports clinicians and researchers who need to explore individual participants and cohort trends without having to work directly from raw files.

The result combines cleaned datasets, exploratory visualizations, Quarto-based statistical reporting, and a Shiny dashboard that brings related data sources into one approachable interface.

Approach

A modular, rerunnable analysis pipeline

I began with data cleaning and preprocessing in Python, then moved the pipeline to R with renv for environment management. Raw files are cleaned independently, standardized through shared helpers, and merged into analysis-ready tables.

Those tables power both the reporting site and dashboard, so every downstream output is based on the same processed data. A Makefile organizes data cleaning, statistical rendering, and application launch into repeatable steps; GitHub Pages refreshes the statistical site automatically.
