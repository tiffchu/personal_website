Overview

Finding patterns in lived ICU-survivor experiences

This independent research project, completed with supervision and data from the UBC School of Nursing, explored computational methods for analyzing qualitative health data. It uses NLP techniques to examine intensive care unit survivor transcripts.

The work sought to identify challenges people experience after hospitalization that might contribute to an unplanned readmission. The research forms part of a qualitative study led by Dr. Fuchsia Howard.

Approach

Latent Dirichlet Allocation for qualitative transcripts

The analysis included data cleaning and conversion, Latent Dirichlet Allocation modelling, and visualization to derive themes from the transcripts. The project was developed in Jupyter notebooks and paired with written analysis.

The technical stack included Gensim, spaCy, pyLDAvis, and scikit-learn. Coherence scores and perplexity metrics supported evaluation of the topic model.
