Overview

Surfacing transactions that warrant closer inspection

This project investigates unusual or statistically significant patterns in financial transactions associated with members of the U.S. Senate. The goal is to use anomaly detection to highlight outliers in public disclosures for closer investigation.

Approach

From public disclosures to unsupervised models

The pipeline extracts and cleans publicly available financial disclosure data, then engineers features that describe investor behavior and incorporate S&P market data. Hierarchical clustering and LSTM autoencoders identify unusual activity.

Interactive plots and documented notebooks make the results easier to inspect and explain.
